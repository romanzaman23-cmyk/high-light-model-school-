Message from the Principal

Welcome to Highlight Model School, Saraghundi Thall — an educational home dedicated to moral discipline, scholastic rigor, and intellectual discovery.

At Highlight Model School, our sacred mission is to guide young students from their foundational early years up through high school matriculation with an unwavering commitment to quality. We blend the official KPK curriculum with modern STEM learning, biometric discipline, and moral mentorship so that our children grow to be leaders of their community and nation.

Our doors are open to every parent striving for a brighter, honorable future for their son and daughter. We welcome you to visit our campus in Saraghundi Thall and join the Highlight family.
